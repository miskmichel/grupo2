THE_PLANTED_TRAP

step-01-eda.md §4.1

michelmisk@vps:~/hackaton-classificacao$ grep -c "^' '$" <(cut -d, -f21 Telco-Customer-Churn.csv)

The assignment warns that TotalCharges “has text type, not numeric — there is a reason for this. Investigate before converting.” The reason turned out to be sharper than generic dirty data.

  • 11values are a literal single space ' ' — not an empty string, which is exactly why the column reads as text
  • 0of them are caught by .isnull() on read
  • 11/11have tenure == 0 — and they are the only 11 rows in the whole dataset with zero tenure
  • 11/11have Churn == 'No' — zero churners among them
  • 10+1contracts: ten Two year, one One year. MonthlyCharges spans 19.70–80.85
THE DIAGNOSIS
impute 0, not the median 1397.47

They are brand-new customers who have never been billed. Assigning them the median lifetime spend of a ~29-month customer invents about 1400 currency units of history and breaks the near-deterministic TotalCharges ~= tenure × MonthlyCharges relation (R² = 0.9991) for exactly those rows. Dropping them is defensible at 0.16% of the data, but silently deletes 11 non-churners in one direction.

# the naive route
>>> df['TotalCharges'].astype(float)

to float: ' '

# what we do instead
df['TotalCharges'] = pd.to_numeric(
    df['TotalCharges'], errors='coerce'
).fillna(0)

[!] PANDAS 3.0 GOTCHA

Under the pinned pandas 3.0.5, this column's dtype is str, not object — pandas 3.0 promotes the dedicated string dtype to the default. Every consequence above is unchanged, but a guard written as dtype == 'object' silently never fires. Dispatch on not is_numeric_dtype(...) instead, as src/data_prep.py does. Essentially every published tutorial for this dataset predates pandas 3 and says object.

TOP DRIVERS — DELIBERATELY EMPTY

No feature-importance chart for this card, and that omission is itself a finding, not a gap: neither KNN nor Naive Bayes yields a natural feature ranking. KNN has no coefficients or splits to read; Naive Bayes's per-feature log-likelihood ratios are not comparable across a mixed Gaussian/Bernoulli model and are inflated by the independence violations that are supposed to make NB struggle here — inventing a ranking would be worse than reporting none. On that independence assumption: the most correlated binary pair within the No-churn class is StreamingMovies_Yes <-> StreamingTV_Yes at r=0.557, and letting Naive Bayes's diagonal covariance loosen to a shared full covariance (LDA) recovers CV ROC-AUC 0.8008 against plain GaussianNB's 0.7746 — evidence the independence assumption really is costing this family something, just not enough to keep the best (discretised) variant off the leaderboard.
